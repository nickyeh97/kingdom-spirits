## Changes (`art/blender/lion_adult.py`)

1. **Mane ring** (metaball `Mane`, accent slot = darker fur): two staggered rings framing the face, placed 10 cm behind the head centre so the ears poke out in front; no coverage of the back of the head or neck (v001 lesson). Spacing / radius ≈ 1.7 so lobes fuse into a ring but keep a fluffy scalloped edge; alternating sizes avoid a bead-string look. Metaball target raised to 1,500 triangles.
2. **Neck sphere removed.** Neck now part of the body loft: three rows added to the front of the profile (reference chest rising into the head), so there is no seam ring. The mane's lower arc covers the throat. Scarf not added (option kept for later).
3. **Continuous light underside:** new point attribute `light` (0–1) on the body: underside (normal z) and lower chest front (normal y, below z ≈ 1.0) blend toward the secondary slot in finalize. Chest and Belly ellipsoids removed.
4. **Power:** width multiplier along the body — chest × 1.22, waist × 0.98, hips × 1.10 (broad chest and shoulders, tucked waist); shoulder ellipsoids bigger, pushed out and forward; thicker thighs; front legs thicker (bevel 0.10, radii up ~12 %); bigger paws.

## Bug found: v005 body was inside out

The hand-ordered loft faces produced inward normals. Effects: in an engine with back-face culling the body would disappear; the normal-based `shade` was upside down (spine light, belly dark). Fix: `bmesh.ops.recalc_face_normals` in `loft()`. New verify check: every closed part of a mesh must have positive signed volume (open fragments such as curve caps are skipped). It fails `Lion_Adult_v005.blend` and passes v006, Lion Baby v004 and the Lamb meshes.

## Pipeline changes

- `finalize_export.py`: `light` attribute (blend toward secondary) before `shade`.
- `verify_blend.py`: outward-normals check.
